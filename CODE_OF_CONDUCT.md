# Code of conduct

Help keep leet welcoming and useful for people of every background and experience level.

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect differing perspectives, boundaries, and accessibility needs.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, and personal attacks.
- Do not publish another person’s private information without permission.
- Keep project spaces focused on collaboration and learning.

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces, including interactions where someone represents the project.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when conduct violates these expectations. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and protect those affected.

To report a conduct concern privately, open a [private report](https://github.com/Noelithub77/leet/security/advisories/new), prefix the title with “Conduct concern,” and include the relevant links and context. This contact route is also used for security reports; conduct concerns will be handled as community matters. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue.
